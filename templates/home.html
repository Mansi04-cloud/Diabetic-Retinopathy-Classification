ABOUT PROJECT

Problem:

Diabetic Retinopathy is a complication of diabetes that damages the retina. If not detected early, it can lead to blindness.

Solution:

This project uses Deep Learning to classify retinal images into different stages of Diabetic Retinopathy through a web application.

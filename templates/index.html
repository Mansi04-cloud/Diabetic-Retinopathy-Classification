ABOUT PROJECT

Problem: Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is a serious complication of diabetes that affects the blood vessels of the retina. If not detected at an early stage, it may lead to permanent vision loss or blindness. Manual screening of retinal fundus images is time-consuming and requires expert ophthalmologists.

Solution: This project uses Deep Learning and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) to automatically classify retinal images into five stages: No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, and Proliferative DR.

The web application provides a secure login and registration system. After authentication, users can upload a fundus image and instantly receive the predicted stage along with confidence score. This system helps in early detection, faster screening, and supports medical professionals in decision making.
